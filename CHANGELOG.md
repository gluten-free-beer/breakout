## [0.1.0] — 2026-07-27

### Added

- Public directory for person and company records.
- JSON-driven person and company profiles.
- Sanitized example records with live installation data ignored by Git.
- Clean Apache routes.
- Dynamic person and company routing without a hardcoded profile slug.
- Record Log timeline with responsive images.
- RSS feed with image enclosures.
- Company social links and YouTube embeds.
- Printable professional records.
- Self-verified status and `#overworking` profile ribbon.
- Representative company imagery with credits and disclaimers.
- Support for project, experience, education, milestone, team, fundraising, and featured-system records.

### Changed

- Replaced conventional professional-network styling with bureaucratic blandness.
